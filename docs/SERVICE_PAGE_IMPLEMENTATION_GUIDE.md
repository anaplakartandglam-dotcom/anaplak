Anaplak Service Page Sections

Hero section — service name, city/location modifier, short benefit-led intro, primary CTA like “Book Now” or “Get Pricing.”

Service overview — what it includes, who it is for, expected result, and why someone would choose it.

Pricing and duration — starting price or range, approximate time, and any package/tier explanation to reduce hesitation.

Before/after or portfolio gallery — labeled images, ideally grouped by service type, because visual proof improves clarity and supports SEO.

Process or what to expect — steps, preparation, aftercare, and realistic expectations.

Social proof — testimonials, review snippets, and result photos tied to that exact service.

Team/expert spotlight — featured stylist or artist, certifications, specialty, and direct booking link where relevant.

FAQs — duration, suitability, maintenance, pre-care, aftercare, and price-related concerns.

A practical CTA stack for spa and beauty pages is:

Primary CTA above the fold: “Book [Service Name].”

Mid-page CTA after pricing/process: “Check Availability” or “Get Consultation.”

Sticky mobile CTA: call, WhatsApp, or book-now button, since many salon visitors arrive on mobile.

Final CTA near FAQs/testimonials: short form for users not ready to book instantly.

Location and trust signals — address, service area, Google Map, business hours, and review proof for local SEO.
